👩‍🏫 Teacher Flow
  1. Click Teacher Dashboard
  2. View concept health bars
  3. Tap student dots for AI insights
  4. Click Create Quiz to build a quiz
  5. Share with students — they take the quiz
  6. Hit Refresh to see updated data
👦 Student Flow
  1. Pick your Language
  2. Enter your Name, Roll No, Subject
  3. Answer 7 animated questions
  4. Tap 🔊 Listen to hear the question
  5. Tap 🎙️ to speak your answer
  6. View Analysis with AI insights
🔊 Audio / Voice
TTS (Listen button): Works in Chrome. If your language voice isn't installed, English plays as fallback.

STT (Speak button): Tap the mic, speak clearly. Say "A", "B", "C", or "D" — or speak the option text.

For native voices: Go to System Settings → Accessibility → Speech or Language → install voice pack.
⏱ Timer & AI Flags
Each question has a countdown (30s default). BodhAI tracks:

⚡ Too Fast (<3s): May be guessing — flagged for verification.

⏱ Timeout: Time ran out — concept needs more practice.

Both flags appear in Student Analysis and Teacher Dashboard.
🗄 How IndexedDB Works
IndexedDB is a real offline database built into your browser. Every time a student completes a quiz, their session (name, subject, score, timing, AI flags) is saved to this database — no internet needed. The Teacher Dashboard reads all sessions from IndexedDB in real time. Sessions persist even when you close and reopen the browser.
